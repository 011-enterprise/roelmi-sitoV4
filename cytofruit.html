About the product A new generation of cosmetic water

CytoFruit® is a line of fruit-derived active waters formulated to replace demineralized water in cosmetics. Obtained exclusively through natural processes from pure juices — without flash distillation or microwave treatment — they are microbiologically pure and enrich the formula with oligoelements and bioactive compounds released through a natural biological filter. The result is a water phase that is significantly more biologically active than standard distilled water, confirmed by in vitro testing.
