Our Sustainability Approach NIP: Nature is people

Sustainability is one of the key drivers of innovation from ROELMI Group's philosophy. A dedicated internal program has been established that combines innovative technologies with circular economy practices. Our daily work and collaboration with the agricultural supply chain are aimed at using byproducts to develop high-performance compounds.
