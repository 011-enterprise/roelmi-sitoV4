About us The story behind a name

We are a family-owned company with an international business.

  • Since 2000
  • Headquarters Origgio, near Milan
  • Offices France, USA, China, South Korea

The name ROELMI is the combination of the names Rosella Malanchin, and the daughters Eleonora and Michela Santus. Through a play on letters and sounds, the name came to represent a strong sense of identity and the ambition to grow, expand and bring the Group’s vision to an international audience. HPC stands for Health and Personal Care, the two main areas in which we operate.

Our headquarters are located in Origgio, near Milan, with commercial offices in both Europe and outside the European Union, including France, the USA, China and South Korea. Our goal is to continue expanding our international presence providing high quality ingredients.

Our work What we do everyday

We specialize in the development of active ingredients and functional ingredients for the personal care market, as well as for the nutraceutical one. Attention to detail is part of our everyday work, together with a strong commitment to quality and respect for the environment.

NIP® Nature is People

In the early 2000s, this approach led to the creation of NIP® (Nature is People). Our intern program is dedicated to sustainability-driven good practices and the relationship between people and the Planet.

Science first Our Approach

We have a specific goal in mind: to develop innovative technologies to optimise the use of natural resources and reduce energy waste for the benefit of society. The protection of biodiversity and the responsible use of natural and renewable resources form an important part of this commitment.

Through responsible innovation and ongoing research and development, we formulate and manufacture ingredients whilst paying particular attention to both people and the environment.

For this reason, every single ingredient is backed by in vitro, in vivo and/or clinical validation, enabling brands on international markets to formulate products supported by robust scientific data, compliant with global regulatory requirements and environmentally friendly.

We aim to conduct our business responsibly and transparently, maintaining high standards of quality and safety while creating a positive impact on people and the environment.

  1. 01

    In vitro

    Cell and tissue models screen efficacy and safety at the earliest stage.

  2. 02

    In vivo

    Studies on human volunteers confirm how the ingredient performs in real use.

  3. 03

    Clinical

    Clinical validation turns those results into data brands can build claims on.
